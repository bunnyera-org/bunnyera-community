\# Community Guidelines



This folder stores BunnyEra Community rules, moderation principles, and participation guidelines.



\## Purpose



Community Guidelines help keep BunnyEra Community safe, useful, respectful, and productive.



\## Core Principles



BunnyEra Community should be:



\- Helpful

\- Respectful

\- Practical

\- Safe

\- Public-friendly

\- Developer-friendly

\- AI-assisted but human-reviewed



\## Allowed Topics



Community members can discuss:



\- BunnyEra products

\- AI agents

\- Workflows

\- Prompts

\- Plugins

\- Themes

\- Development questions

\- Product feedback

\- Ecommerce operations

\- Automation ideas

\- Public tutorials



\## Not Allowed



Do not post:



\- API keys

\- Passwords

\- Private tokens

\- 2FA secrets

\- Customer private data

\- Payment information

\- Identity documents

\- Unauthorized copyrighted assets

\- Malware or harmful code

\- Harassment or abusive content

\- Spam



\## Sharing Rules



When sharing agents, workflows, prompts, plugins, or themes:



\- Explain the purpose clearly.

\- Include usage instructions.

\- Mention risks or limitations.

\- Do not include secrets.

\- Do not include private business data.

\- Keep content reviewable.



\## Moderator Rules



Moderators should:



\- Remove unsafe content.

\- Warn users when needed.

\- Protect private information.

\- Keep discussions focused.

\- Encourage useful contributions.

\- Escalate serious security issues.
